FAQ

What is Moriachem Kutam?
Moriachem Kutam translates to "Mother Mary's Family" and is the community organization behind Monthi Fest — formed to plan and run the festival each year. It handles ticket sales, donations, coordinates logistics, and purchases.
What is Monthi Fest?
Monthi Fest, celebrates the Nativity of Mother Mary and is a cherished tradition in Konkani Catholic communities. It includes offering the season's first harvest and flowers to Mary in thanksgiving, followed by Mass, a procession, and community celebration with food, music, and dance. Our celebration here in Central Texas carries this heritage forward, bringing our community together in faith and joy.
What is Novem Jehvonn?
Novem Jehvonn means "new meal" in Konkani — it's the traditional feast prepared from the season's first harvest, blessed as part of the Monthi Fest celebration. Families and the community come together to share this meal as an act of thanksgiving for the harvest and God's blessings, making it one of the most cherished parts of the celebration.
What kind of food is served?
The feast is entirely vegetarian, following the traditional Novem Jevan menu. As per custom, the dishes are served in an odd number — traditionally considered auspicious for celebratory occasions. Some dishes you can expect: Alun Dento, Gosalay Thel Piyao, Thendli Channa Sukha etc.
Who is welcome to attend?
Everyone is welcome to attend — Monthi Fest is open to the entire community, families, and friends of all backgrounds.
